NEON INC

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short: Neon Inc makes free web apps: Scent Scout, Neon Arcade and Neon Gym. If you sign in with Google, we keep your name, email address, profile picture and your app progress so you can use it on any device. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

Neon Inc is a small, independent maker of free apps, run from South Africa. The apps live at neoninc.github.io. You can reach us at neoninc.support@gmail.com.

Using the apps without signing in

You don't need an account. Without signing in, everything you do (your arcade progress, your gym log and your fragrance watchlist) is saved only in your browser on your own device. It is never sent to us.

What we collect when you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares these basic details with us:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile picture
  • a Google account ID that tells us it's you

We don't ask for anything else from your Google account. We can't see your password, contacts, files, emails or any other Google data.

After you sign in, we also keep what you save in the apps, so it's there on any device:

  • Neon Arcade: your player name, avatar, level, scores, achievements, rewards and settings.
  • Neon Gym: your training plan, workouts and sets, body weight and waist entries, food log and targets, and the details you enter to work out your targets (sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal).
  • Scent Scout: your watchlist of fragrances and the prices when you saved them.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your name and picture in the apps.
  • To save your progress and bring it back when you sign in on another phone or computer.

That's all. We don't use your information for advertising, we don't build profiles about you, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone. Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where it's stored and who can see it

Your sign-in and saved progress are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), on Google's servers. Each person's information is kept separately, and the apps only let you read and change your own.

The person who runs Neon Inc can access the stored information through the Firebase console, but only looks at it when needed to keep the apps working, fix a problem, or carry out a request from you. We don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

Other services the apps use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the websites.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering.
  • GitHub's public API supplies the "updated" times shown on the home page.

Like any website, these services receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your device loads files from them. Scent Scout links to shops and to Fragrantica. When you open those links, those sites' own privacy policies apply.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account and saved progress until you ask us to delete them. Signing out doesn't delete anything. It just stops syncing on that device.

To delete your account and everything saved with it, email neoninc.support@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with. We'll delete it within 30 days and let you know when it's done. You can also ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it.

You can remove Neon Inc's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account's third-party connections page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Children

The apps aren't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has signed in, email us and we'll delete their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also show a notice on the home page.
